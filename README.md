# QR2Go

## What Is This?
QR2Go helps you create, save, and reuse QR codes for websites, contacts, Wi-Fi, messages, locations, and more.

## Features

- Generate QR codes for websites, contacts, Wi-Fi networks, email, SMS, phone calls, events, and locations.
- Preview QR output before saving, including scan-safety validation and readability warnings.
- Save generated QR export cards to Photos.
- Customize QR appearance with foreground and background colors, an optional center logo, and export album settings.
- Pick contacts from the address book and fill location QR codes from the current location or a map picker.
- Save favorite QR codes for reuse, then rename, reopen, or delete them later.
- Launch saved favorites from home screen quick actions.
- Generate QR exports from the share extension for shared URLs, text, contacts, and vCards.
